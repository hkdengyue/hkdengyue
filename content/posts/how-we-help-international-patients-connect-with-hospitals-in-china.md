# How We Help International Patients Connect with Hospitals in China

![Professional China Cross-border Medical Services](/images/china-cross-border-medical-services-cover.png)

As China's healthcare system continues to advance, an increasing number of international patients are considering traveling to China for specialized medical treatment, including [cancer care](https://hkdengyue.github.io/cancer/), complex surgeries, [innovative therapies](https://dengyuemed.com/news/may-2026-china-innovative-drug-approvals/), rare disease management, and second medical opinions.

However, for most overseas patients, the greatest challenge is not deciding whether to seek treatment in China—it is knowing **how to connect with the right hospital, the right specialists, and the appropriate treatment pathway**.

At **DengYueMed**, we provide professional [cross-border medical coordination services](https://dengyuemed.com/blog/cancer-treatment-in-china-for-international-patients/) that help international patients access China's leading healthcare institutions through an efficient, transparent, and patient-centered process.

# Our Cross-Border Medical Coordination Process

## Step 1: Initial Consultation and Needs Assessment

Every patient's medical journey begins with a comprehensive consultation.

We work closely with patients and their families to understand:

- Current diagnosis
- Medical history
- Previous treatments
- Current symptoms
- Treatment objectives
- Country of residence
- Desired treatment timeline

Rather than immediately recommending hospitals, we first evaluate the patient's clinical needs to determine whether they may benefit from:

- Specialized oncology centers
- Neurosurgical expertise
- Innovative targeted therapies
- Clinical trials
- Multidisciplinary treatment (MDT)

---

## Step 2: Medical Record Organization and Translation

Medical records from different countries often vary significantly in language, format, and terminology.

Our team assists with:

- Medical record organization
- Treatment timeline reconstruction
- Imaging report classification
- Pathology report review
- Professional English-Chinese medical translation
- Clinical summary preparation

This allows Chinese physicians to efficiently review each patient's medical history before consultation.

---

## Step 3: Matching the Right Hospitals and Specialists

China has thousands of hospitals, but each institution has different areas of expertise.

Instead of simply recommending famous hospitals, we identify the most appropriate hospital-specialist combination based on each patient's condition.

Matching considers:

- Disease specialty
- Physician expertise
- Hospital capabilities
- Appointment availability
- Treatment feasibility
- Innovative therapy accessibility

Examples include:

- [Oncology](https://dengyuemed.github.io/oncology/) → National cancer centers
- Neurosurgery → Specialized brain tumor centers
- Rare diseases → National referral hospitals
- Precision medicine → Academic medical centers

---

## Step 4: Specialist Appointment and Medical Consultation

After selecting the most suitable hospital, we assist patients with:

- International Patient Center registration
- Specialist outpatient appointments
- Multidisciplinary Team (MDT) consultations
- Online medical consultations
- Pre-arrival medical evaluations

Patients receive:

- Initial treatment recommendations
- Diagnostic review
- Treatment feasibility assessment
- Personalized treatment planning

---

## Step 5: Planning the Medical Journey

Traveling abroad for treatment requires much more than simply booking a flight.

We help coordinate:

- Hospital admission
- Bed reservations
- Appointment scheduling
- Medical invitation letters
- Visa support documents
- Interpreter arrangements
- Transportation planning
- Accommodation recommendations

Our goal is to minimize delays so patients can begin treatment as soon as possible after arriving in China.

---

## Step 6: In-Hospital Coordination

Once treatment begins, our support continues throughout the patient's stay.

Our services include:

- Medical interpretation
- Communication between doctors and patients
- Appointment coordination
- Examination scheduling
- Treatment procedure assistance
- Family communication support

For patients receiving long-term therapies such as chemotherapy, immunotherapy, targeted therapy, or radiotherapy, we also help coordinate ongoing treatment schedules.

---

## Step 7: Follow-up After Returning Home

Medical care does not end when the patient leaves the hospital.

We continue supporting patients through:

- Discharge summary translation
- Medication guidance
- Follow-up consultation coordination
- Remote physician communication
- Future treatment planning
- Return-to-China arrangements when necessary

This helps patients maintain continuity of care even after returning to their home countries.
